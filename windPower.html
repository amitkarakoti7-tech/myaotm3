What is Wind Power?

Wind Power is a renewable source of energy created by converting the kinetic energy of moving air into electricity. Large wind turbines rotate when wind blows, and a generator converts this motion into electrical power. Wind energy is clean, sustainable, and produces electricity without burning fossil fuels.

📅 History

Windmills have been used for over 2,000 years. Modern wind turbines became popular after the 1970s.

⚙️ How It Works

Wind → Blades → Rotor → Generator → Electricity Grid.

🌿 Benefits

Produces clean electricity with zero fuel cost and very low carbon emissions.

🚀 Future

Offshore wind farms and AI-powered turbines are making renewable energy smarter.
